About

We buy, grade, and move
hard to find inventory.

Established 2021·Atlanta, GA·Minneapolis, MN

JML Enterprises LLC is a multi-brand holding company operating out of Atlanta, Georgia and Minneapolis, Minnesota. We run each brand independently, with its own sourcing, its own channels, and its own buyers.

Our largest operation is AtlantaJake, a vintage, Japanese designer, and deadstock luxury menswear business. Alongside it we run isellleds, a specialized automotive lighting retailer selling through eBay and authorized dealer channels.

This page is written for the people who need to know whether we are a real operation before they ship us goods or buy a pallet from us. If that is you, everything below is the short version. Everything else is a conversation.

How We Operate

We are a buying and grading operation before we are a retailer. Inventory comes in by the container and the case, gets sorted, authenticated, photographed, and then goes out through whichever channel fits the piece.

A single item might sell retail on Depop, at a higher price on Grailed, or move as part of a hundred-piece wholesale bundle, depending on what it is and who is asking. That flexibility is the point. It means we can take large mixed lots that a single-channel seller has to pass on.

Sourcing

We source from the United States, Mexico, Europe, and Japan, with wholesale shipments arriving on a quarterly cycle. Mexico is a major part of our volume and where a lot of our American vintage comes back through. The Japanese pipeline is the one most US sellers do not have, and it is where the pieces our buyers actually chase come from.

What we look for

  • Japanese designer and streetwear. Undercover, Kapital, Needles, Comme des Garçons, Number (N)ine, Hysteric Glamour, Bape, Visvim, WTAPS, Neighborhood.
  • Pre-owned and deadstock luxury. European houses and archive-tier designer, condition permitting.
  • American vintage. Polo Ralph Lauren, Levi's, Carhartt, Champion, Harley Davidson, nineties graphic and sportswear.
  • Mixed lots. We buy unsorted. Sorting is our job, not yours.

If you are a supplier holding volume in any of the above, we buy outright and we do not ask you to grade it first.

Standards

  • Authentication. Every designer and luxury piece is checked before it is listed or bundled. We do not pass questionable goods downstream.
  • Photography in house. Professional studio setup, shot in monthly batches, multiple angles and detail shots per item. No stock photos, no borrowed listings.
  • Honest condition grading. Flaws are photographed and stated. A buyer who trusts the grade buys again.
  • Consistent listing data. Brand, era, measurements, and construction details recorded at intake, not guessed at later.

isellleds

Our automotive lighting brand, operating on eBay. We sell headlights, LED conversions, and lighting upgrades as an authorized reseller rather than a grey-market importer, which matters in a category where counterfeit product is common and warranties are worth something.

Product lines. AlphaRex, Morimoto, Diode Dynamics, and Oracle Lighting, alongside Sylvania.

Distribution. We work through established lighting distributors and are expanding our authorized dealer coverage.

Shops and installers buying at volume should reach out directly rather than ordering through the eBay storefront.
