Instagram Content Manager

AtlantaJake·Contract·Remote

AtlantaJake is a vintage, designer, and streetwear brand based in Atlanta. We source from the US, Europe, and Japan, and we sell to people who know exactly what they are looking at.

We are looking for someone to run our Instagram on a fixed weekly schedule. Two posts a week. All content is provided. Your job is turning it into finished posts with the right captions, hashtags, and formatting.

BrandAtlantaJake
TypeContract, remote
ScheduleTwo posts weekly, Monday and Friday
PayPer post, rates discussed on application
BasedAtlanta, GA

The Work

  • Build one themed post per drop from photography we supply. Hats, tees, footwear, outerwear.
  • Write captions in our voice. Direct, knowledgeable, no filler.
  • Research hashtags that reach both broad streetwear and niche designer audiences.
  • Publish on schedule, Monday and Friday.
  • Optional: shoot original content for a higher rate.
  • Optional: produce Instagram Stories, paid per story.

Who We Are Looking For

Strong knowledge of designer and streetwear culture. Someone who understands the language, the brands, and the community.

  • Familiarity with Japanese labels is a significant plus. Undercover, Kapital, Needles, Comme des Garçons, Number (N)ine, Hysteric Glamour, Bape.
  • Comfortable identifying eras, collaborations, and what makes a piece worth talking about.
  • Reliable on a fixed schedule, every week.
  • Resale platform experience is a bonus. Depop, Grailed, eBay.

Apply

Send a short note about what you wear, collect, or follow, along with any account you run or have worked on. A portfolio is welcome but not required.

Apply by Email

Full role details and terms are sent to applicants directly.
